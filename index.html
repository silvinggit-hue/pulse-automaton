Pulse Automaton

Pulse Automaton is a private, local automation tool used by an individual creator to assist with the creation and publication of short-form videos to the creator's own YouTube channel.

The application is not offered as a public service and does not manage third-party YouTube channels.

Pulse Automaton uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • identify the authenticated YouTube channel,
  • upload videos explicitly reviewed and approved by the operator,
  • verify upload and publication status, and
  • help prevent accidental duplicate uploads.

All public uploads require explicit human review and approval.

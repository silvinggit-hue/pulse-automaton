Pulse Automaton Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Pulse Automaton is a private, local automation tool operated by an individual creator for managing the creator's own YouTube channel.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

Pulse Automaton uses YouTube API Services.

The application may access:

  • YouTube channel ID and channel title,
  • upload-related metadata,
  • video upload and publication status.

2. Purpose of Data Use

The data is used only to:

  • confirm the authenticated YouTube channel,
  • upload videos explicitly approved by the operator,
  • verify upload and publication status,
  • prevent accidental duplicate uploads.

3. Data Storage

OAuth tokens and upload-session information are stored locally on the operator's computer in encrypted form.

Sensitive OAuth credentials are not stored in the public source repository.

4. Data Sharing

Pulse Automaton does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties.

5. Third-Party Access

Pulse Automaton is not offered to third-party users and does not manage unrelated creators' YouTube accounts.

6. User Control and Revocation

The operator may revoke the application's access at any time through their Google Account security settings.
